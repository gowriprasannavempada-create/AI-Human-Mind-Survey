# AI Technologies and Human Mind - Survey Analysis

##  Project Overview

This project analyzes how people use Artificial Intelligence (AI) and how AI may influence human thinking, productivity, creativity, trust, and decision-making.

The data was collected through a Google Form survey with **50 responses**. The responses were exported into a CSV file and analyzed using Python.

The main goal of this project is to understand people's opinions, usage patterns, trust levels, concerns, and views about the future impact of AI.

---

##  Objectives

- Analyze how frequently people use AI tools.
- Identify the most commonly used AI tools.
- Understand the main reasons people use AI.
- Study the perceived impact of AI on the human mind.
- Analyze the relationship between AI usage and productivity.
- Study people's trust in AI-generated information.
- Analyze whether people verify AI-generated information.
- Identify major concerns about AI.
- Identify the benefits people associate with AI.
- Understand people's opinions about the future impact of AI.
- Perform basic statistical and visual analysis using Python.

---

## Data Analysis Performed

### 1. Dataset Inspection

The dataset was loaded and examined for:

- Number of rows and columns
- Data types
- Missing values
- Duplicate records

### 2. AI Usage Analysis

The project analyzes:

- AI tools used by respondents
- Frequency of AI usage
- Reasons for using AI

### 3. Human Mind Analysis

The survey responses were analyzed to understand perceived effects of AI on:

- Productivity
- Creativity
- Independent thinking
- Dependency on AI

### 4. Trust and Verification Analysis

The project studies:

- How much respondents trust AI-generated information
- Whether respondents verify AI-generated information
- The relationship between verification behavior and trust levels

A numerical trust score was created:

| Response | Score |
|---|---:|
| Never | 1 |
| Rarely | 2 |
| Sometimes | 3 |
| Mostly | 4 |
| Always | 5 |

### 5. AI Concerns

The project identifies common concerns respondents have about AI.

### 6. AI Benefits

The project analyzes the benefits respondents associate with AI.

### 7. Future Impact

The survey responses were analyzed to understand people's views about the future impact of AI.

### 8. Correlation Analysis

A correlation heatmap was created to study relationships between selected numerical variables.

### 9. Open-Ended Responses

Respondents' personal opinions about AI were also examined.
